What is Bachata Factory Helsinki (BFH)?

BFH aims to advance bachata dancers filled with the desire to improve their dancing skills. Instead of attending classes, in BFH the learning comes from the participants themselves. Through peer teaching activities, the group helps its members with new points of view, constructive feedback, new dancing concepts, a focus on narrow aspects of the dance, challenges, and exercises.

Occasionally, teachers and international artists are invited with a specific theme and focus, to contribute their expertise to the members’ dancing growth.

When and where is BFH held?

BFH is held weekly, every Friday, at OddSpace Vallila. The first project starts in mid-September 2026 and finishes by the end of November.

Depending on its success and the funds available, there will be more projects and batches, and the activities will continue and possibly spread to multiple groups.

Who can take part in this project?

In the interest of focus and cooperation between team members, this project is aimed at a restricted group of 16 to 20 dancers. Only the people chosen by the organiser board can take part in BFH.

It is possible to be invited as a guest for one-off events. Guests are chosen from the waiting list: if you would like to join it, please leave your full name and WhatsApp number below.
