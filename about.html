What is Bachata Factory Helsinki (BFH)?

BFH aims to advance bachata dancers filled with the desire to improve their dancing skills. Instead of attending classes, in BFH the learning comes from the participants themselves. Through peer teaching activities, the group helps its members with new points of view, constructive feedback, new dancing concepts, a focus on narrow aspects of the dance, challenges, and exercises.

Occasionally, teachers and international artists are invited with a specific theme and focus, to contribute their expertise to the members’ dancing growth.

When and where is BFH held?

BFH is held weekly in Vallila. The first project will start in mid-September and finish by the end of November.

Who can take part in this project?

To improve the focus and cooperation between team members, this project is aimed at a restricted group of 16 to 20 dancers. Only the people who have been nominated can take part in BFH.
